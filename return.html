Return, Refund & Delivery Policy

Humari koshish hamesha yeh rehti hai ki aapko behtareen product mile. Lekin agar delivery ke baad koi samasya aati hai, toh aap neeche diye gaye niyamo ke tahat madad le sakte hain:

1. Return & Replacement (Wapsi aur Badlaav)

  • Aap product milne ke 3 se 7 dinon ke andar return ya replacement ke liye request kar sakte hain.
  • Product original packaging, tags, aur unused condition mein hona chahiye.

2. Refund Process (Pese Wapsi)

  • Online payment karne par refund aapke original payment source par 3-5 working days mein bhej diya jata hai.
  • COD (Cash on Delivery) ke mamle mein, customer ki bank details ya UPI ID lekar refund transfer kiya jata hai.

3. Damaged / Defective Items

  • Agar tula hua ya kharab product mila hai, toh unboxing ki video ya photo ke sath turant humein report karein.
