Refund Issue — Paisa Kaise Check Karein?

Banking cycle & refund status
▼

Agar aapne order cancel ya return kiya hai aur refund ka intezar kar rahe hain, toh yeh steps follow karein:

1. Banking Processing Time UPI/Netbanking refunds me 24 se 48 working hours lagte hain. Bank holidays par process agle working day shuru hota hai.
2. Bank Statement / Mini Statement Check Kayi baar bank ka SMS alert miss ho jata hai. Apne Bank App me jakar statement check karein.
3. Cash on Delivery (COD) Refund COD orders ka refund aapke diye gaye UPI ID ya Bank Details par sidha transfer hota hai.

Product Replace Kaise Karein? (Kharab / Damage)

Testing steps & 7-day replacement
▼

Agar LED bulb nahi jal raha ya sensor kaam nahi kar raha, toh replacement lene se pehle yeh 2 test zaroor karein:

1. Holder & Wiring Check Bulb ko ghar ke kisi doosre holder me laga kar check karein taaki holder ki loose wiring verify ho sake.
2. Radar Motion Light Test Radar motion bulb daylight me auto-off rehta hai. Isko andhere kamre me movement karke test karein.
3. 7 Days Free Replacement Delivery ke 7 dino ke andar defective/damaged piece par 100% free replacement milta hai.
